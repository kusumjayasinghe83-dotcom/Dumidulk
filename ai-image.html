AI Image Prompt Mastery

A good image prompt has 5 parts: subject, style, lighting, camera/lens and quality. Copy the examples below and change the subject to make them yours.

Cinematic portrait

Ultra-detailed cinematic portrait of a young man in a rain-soaked neon city street at night, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, soft rim light, teal and orange color grading, 8k, photorealistic.

Product photo

Professional product photo of a matte black wireless headphone on a marble surface, soft studio lighting, subtle reflection, minimal background, high-end commercial advertising style.

Fantasy landscape

Epic fantasy landscape with floating islands, waterfalls falling into clouds, golden hour sunlight, volumetric light rays, highly detailed digital painting, wide angle.

Cute 3D character

Cute 3D cartoon baby panda wearing a tiny yellow raincoat, soft pastel background, Pixar-style rendering, glossy eyes, smooth lighting, high detail.

YouTube thumbnail background

Bold YouTube thumbnail background, dramatic red and black gradient, light burst in the center, empty space on the left for text, high contrast, 16:9.

Prompt formula

[Subject] + [Action / pose] + [Environment] + [Style] + [Lighting] + [Camera / lens] + [Quality words]
